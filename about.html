Our Story

A Legacy Since 1948

Assam Engineering Institute was first established as the "Assam Civil Engineering School" in 1948. Located in the heart of Guwahati, it is one of the premier technical institutions in all of Northeast India.

Affiliated to the State Council for Technical Education (SCTE), Assam and approved by AICTE, AEI today offers diploma programmes across six engineering disciplines, producing skilled engineers who serve industries across India and abroad.

  • AICTE approved & affiliated to SCTE, Assam
  • Fully residential campus with hostel, library & sports facilities
  • MOU signed with NIELIT for skill training
  • Strong industry linkages for placements and internships
